📘 B-Sim AI Prototype User Manual & Interactive Guide

Welcome to B-Sim AI

B-Sim AI is a state-of-the-art business simulation platform that teaches macroeconomic strategy, corporate finance, operational sustainability, and workforce logistics. This interactive guide walks you through the active flows, user roles, and core functions implemented in this high-fidelity prototype. Teammates and evaluators can use the action buttons below to jump directly to any active screen.

👥 User Roles & Profiles

The platform is structured around three distinct user roles, each with fully isolated preferences, alerts, and settings profiles:

  • Student (Jamie Dela Cruz): Accesses the active company dashboard, evaluates economic briefing statistics, sets dynamic allocation policies, and submits quarterly operational choices.
  • Instructor (Dr. Ana Reyes): Creates and initiates simulation sessions, monitors cross-cohort performance stats, overrides scores, and manages active rosters.
  • Institutional Admin: Audits institutional enterprise licensing details, manages credit card payments, configures campus SAML/Google SSO, and views system telemetry logs.

🔄 The Student Strategy Loop

Students navigate through a stateful simulation cycle. A progress stepper at the top of all pages tracks this four-stage flow:

  1. Market Outlook Gate: Analyze macroeconomic indicators and competitor events. Submitting decisions is locked until review is complete.
  2. Decision Submission: Fill in pricing, operations, and sustainability fields with collapsible benchmark stats shown.
  3. Processing Phase: Watch the live 2.5s calculation loader run.
  4. Results & AI Feedback: Review KPI scores (deltas vs. averages) and read Section A (Diagnosis) and Section B (Playbook) recommendations.
1. Outlook Gate → 2. Decision Form → 3. Round Results → 4. AI Feedback →

⚙️ Key Interactive Features

Several premium controls have been integrated to demonstrate modern user experience standards:

  • LMS Sync & SSO Toggles: Try enabling/disabling connections in settings pages. All checkboxes and save/reset behaviors are fully functional.
  • Ethical Sourcing & AI Automation Switches: Replace simple inputs with CSS slider toggle switches.
  • Dynamic Portfolios: Choose Dynamic vs. Conservative strategies to automatically adjust allocation ratios.
  • Collapsible Benchmark Drawer: Open or close details side-panel on the Decision Form to save screen space.
