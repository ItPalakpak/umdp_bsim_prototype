## B. Executive Summary

**Business Idea:** B-Sim AI is an advanced, AI-powered macroeconomic business simulation platform tailored specifically for higher education. Aligning with the theme of strengthening global relationships through sustainable innovation and technology, the platform immerses student teams in a shared virtual economy where they manage simulated companies. It transforms traditional business education into an experiential learning system by requiring students to make real-time strategic decisions across finance, operations, marketing, and human resources, while continuously balancing profitability with mandatory sustainability constraints.

**Target Market:** The primary target market consists of higher education institutions — specifically universities, business schools, and entrepreneurship centers operating within the global B2B EdTech sector. With a strategic focus on the rapidly expanding Asia-Pacific region, including Indonesia's extensive network of 4,400 universities, the platform addresses the growing administrative demand for measurable, practical learning outcomes and the global push to integrate sustainability into core academic curricula.

**Uniqueness:** B-Sim AI fundamentally differentiates itself from static case study methods and traditional simulation games through its proprietary AI-driven evaluation engine. Instead of merely providing quantitative rankings, the AI functions as an intelligent auditor that automatically diagnoses strategic errors — such as inventory imbalances from overproduction or pricing misalignments leading to lost market share — and delivers personalized, qualitative feedback on exactly why specific decisions failed. Furthermore, the platform features a dynamic shared economy where every team's pricing and operational decision directly alters the competitive market conditions for all other participants, seamlessly embedding environmental and social governance as a core survival constraint rather than an optional module.

**Financial Potential:** Operating on a highly scalable B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) model, B-Sim AI generates stable, recurring revenue through flexible usage-based fees ($15–$30 per student) and annual institutional licensing tiers ranging from $3,500 to $15,000. The commercialization roadmap is exceptionally capital efficient, initiating with a lean $7,000 budget to develop a functional prototype over an 8-week sprint. Following successful institutional pilot testing and subsequent seed fundraising, the platform is projected to scale across 80–150 institutions, unlocking a substantial revenue potential of up to $1.5 million by the second year of full commercial operation.

## D. Business Description

### Business Concept

B-Sim AI is an advanced AI-powered business simulation platform designed specifically for higher education institutions. The platform operates by creating a shared virtual economy where student teams directly manage their own companies and make comprehensive strategic decisions across pricing, operations, finance, marketing, and human resources. Directly responding to the competition's theme of sustainable innovation and technology, the system not only simulates real-time macroeconomic volatility but also deeply integrates sustainability constraints into the core of every decision. This approach transforms traditional business education from passive theoretical learning into an experiential learning system, requiring students to continuously analyze financial data to balance short-term profit maximization with long-term sustainable performance.

### Product Uniqueness

The platform's uniqueness lies in its automated evaluation and feedback mechanism driven entirely by AI. Unlike standard simulation games that only provide quantitative scores and rankings, B-Sim AI functions as an intelligent, automated performance audit. After each simulation round, the AI system automatically evaluates every team's decisions, accurately diagnoses strategic errors — such as inventory imbalances from overproduction or pricing misalignments leading to lost market share — and delivers personalized strategic analysis and improvement recommendations for the next stage. Furthermore, because the virtual economy is dynamic and interactive, one team's decisions directly alter the competitive landscape for all others, creating a highly realistic environment that demands continuous adaptation for survival.

### Core Strengths

The core competitive advantage of B-Sim AI is its ability to thoroughly address the limitations of static case-study methods, while significantly optimizing the workload for instructors. Running a business simulation class typically requires massive resources for grading and tracking, but this platform fully automates financial and human resource monitoring. The system continuously tracks key metrics such as revenue, costs, EBITDA, and profit margins, consolidating them into visual dashboards for faculty. This automation completely eliminates manual grading, allowing instructors to efficiently manage large student cohorts, thereby delivering exceptional scalability and strong commercial adoption potential across universities.

## E. Segmentation, Targeting, and Positioning

The platform strategically segments its market into two main institutional categories: higher education institutions (such as universities and business schools focused on formal curricula and long-term graduate outcomes) and innovation ecosystems (including entrepreneurship centers and incubators emphasizing practical venture development). Within these segments, the primary target market is universities and business schools, which perfectly align with the platform's B2B SaaS model. These institutions possess stable annual budgets for educational technology and typically integrate simulation tools directly into their official academic curricula. This structural integration creates high switching costs and robust long-term retention rates across student cohorts, making universities a highly predictable, scalable, and valuable customer base.

In terms of product positioning, B-Sim AI is defined as an experiential, data-driven, and sustainability-focused learning system designed for higher education. Unlike traditional lecture-based methods, standard simulation games, or generic AI tutors, this platform seamlessly combines virtual company management, dynamic market simulation, and real-time financial dashboards into one integrated environment. The platform provides a unique learning loop through AI-driven feedback that not only evaluates financial outcomes but deeply analyzes the strategic intent behind every operational decision. Furthermore, for universities seeking to modernize their business education, the platform fundamentally embeds sustainability into the core decision-making process — rather than treating environmental and social governance as a separate module, the system forces students to continuously balance profitability, market risk, and ethical impact.

## G. Operational Plan

### Service Process — How the Simulation Runs

The platform will operate through a structured simulation cycle where each round represents one business quarter. Instructors will configure the session once; the AI engine will handle all evaluation and feedback automatically throughout. The sustainability layer will be embedded into every decision category, with delayed consequences surfacing in later rounds to replicate how real-world business trade-offs unfold over time.

| Step | Stage | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Platform Setup | The instructor configures rounds, market scenarios, scoring weights, and sustainability constraints for the session. |
| 2 | Team Onboarding | Teams receive virtual companies with identical starting resources and baseline market conditions. |
| 3 | Decision Round Opens | Teams review market briefs and submit decisions across pricing, operations, finance, hiring, and sustainability. |
| 4 | Simulation Engine Processes | The AI engine calculates outcomes for all teams simultaneously, applying competitive dynamics and live market events. |
| 5 | Results & AI Feedback | Each team receives financial results and a personalized critique: what failed, why it happened, and what to adjust next round. |
| 6 | Next Round / Final State | The cycle continues until the final round, producing cumulative rankings and an AI strategic summary per team. |

*Table G.1 — Simulation cycle: six-step operational flow from instructor setup to final round output.*

### Organizational Structure

For the prototype and early-adoption phase, the team will operate as a lean five-function structure. Each role maps directly to a core deliverable, with no redundant layers.

| Role | Responsibilities | Team Size | Tools / Stack |
|---|---|---|---|
| Platform Development | Backend infrastructure, simulation engine, database, API architecture | 2 developers | Node.js / Python, PostgreSQL, REST API |
| AI Integration | Scenario generation logic, decision analysis engine, personalized feedback module | 1–2 developers | Python, LLM API (Anthropic / OpenAI), ML pipelines |
| UI/UX Design | Student dashboard, instructor panel, financial visualizations, mobile responsiveness | 1 designer | Figma, React.js, Tailwind CSS |
| Testing & QA | Functional testing, simulation logic accuracy, performance benchmarking | 1 QA lead | Jest, manual testing protocol |
| Operations & Outreach | University partnerships, onboarding, support, institutional marketing | 1–2 members | CRM, email outreach, demo decks |

*Table G.2 — Functional team structure for prototype and early-adoption phase.*

### Infrastructure and Resources

The platform will be delivered as a cloud-hosted web application accessible from any modern browser and device, requiring no client-side installation from students or institutions. Core infrastructure will run on standard cloud services (AWS, GCP, or Azure) with usage-based pricing, keeping early-stage costs low and allowing the system to scale proportionally with adoption. The AI feedback engine will use a large language model API rather than an in-house trained model, keeping development scope realistic while delivering high-quality, context-aware qualitative output after each simulation round.

### Management Methods

Development across all four phases will follow a sprint-based workflow with each time block mapped to a defined deliverable — from the 8-week prototype build through the Month 19–24 national commercial expansion. All code will be version-controlled through Git with mandatory peer review before any merge. Each new partner university will move through the same four-step onboarding protocol: technical setup, instructor walkthrough, student orientation, and a structured end-of-semester debrief. Debrief feedback will feed directly into the product backlog at every phase, driving sprint priorities and sustaining the 85% retention rate underpinning Year 2 revenue projections.
